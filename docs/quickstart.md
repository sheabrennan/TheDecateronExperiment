# Quickstart

Five minutes from "never seen this" to running your first room.

## 1. Open the app

**[Click here to run it](https://thedecateronexperiment.com/)** — no
install, no account. It runs in your browser and nothing you do leaves your
machine.

## 2. Generate a dungeon

You land on the **library** screen.

1. Give the dungeon a name.
2. Pick a pack — **Field Reference Example** is a small original pack that
   works out of the box and is what these docs' screenshots are taken from.
3. Choose who decides which tesseract a door opens into:
   - **GM picks** — you choose, on the spot, at the table.
   - **Chance picks** — the app rolls it for you.
4. Click **Generate**.

That's a whole 40-room dungeon built in a fraction of a second — every room
placed, every key seeded, nothing left for you to prep.

## 3. Read the room

You're dropped straight into the **room panel**: this is what you, the GM,
see for wherever the party currently stands.

- **Read aloud** — text safe to say to your players verbatim.
- **On a closer look** — what a more careful look reveals, on request.
- **GM notes** — what's actually going on. Never shown to players.
- **Creatures / features** — click to toggle as the party deals with them.

## 4. Open a door

The **Doors** drawer (usually open on the side) shows what's behind each
exit before you commit. Pick one, and the room panel updates to the new
room.

## 5. Save your progress

You don't have to do anything — the game autosaves to this browser as you
play. When you want to move to another device, open the menu (☰) and
**Export** to a `.json` file; **Import save…** on the library screen brings
it back in anywhere.
